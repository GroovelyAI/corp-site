About Groovely

Making dance culture something everyone can join

Groovely AI started with a simple idea: the best dance trends should be for everyone, not just the people who can learn a routine in an afternoon. Our app uses generative video to let anyone step into the moves they see online, using nothing more than a photo.

Groovely is built and operated by SLHDDS LTD, an independent software company based in London, United Kingdom. We take privacy seriously: there are no accounts, no tracking profiles, and your images are used solely to create the videos you ask for.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Groovely?

No. Groovely never asks for a name, email address or password. Install the app from the App Store and you can start creating immediately.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit photo of one person facing the camera. Full-length shots give the AI the most to work with, but upper-body photos work too. The app shows framing guides when you upload.

How does pricing work?

Groovely runs on coins. A subscription tops up your coin balance, and each video you generate spends a few coins. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and can be managed or cancelled at any time in your iPhone settings.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are used solely to generate the videos you request. They are never used to train AI models, never sold or shared for advertising, and can be deleted from within the app. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Who can use Groovely?

Groovely is available for iPhone running iOS 15.1 or later and is intended for users aged 18 and over.
